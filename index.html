About PetaLab

We use spatial insight to see what conventional perspectives often miss.

PetaLab is a nonprofit initiative exploring how space-based technology and spatial data can improve the way we understand economic, environmental, and social development.

We combine research, Earth observation, spatial analysis, and accessible communication to transform complex geographic information into meaningful insight.

Why Space + Place Matter

Development is spatial.

Climate risk, agricultural productivity, environmental change, urban growth, infrastructure, and inequality all happen somewhere.

Maps and Earth observation help reveal patterns and changes that conventional statistics may not fully show.

Satellite → Spatial Data → Pattern → Insight → Action
