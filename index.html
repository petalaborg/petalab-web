Seeing development differently.

PetaLab is a nonprofit research initiative using Earth observation and spatial data to understand how climate, agriculture, and cities are changing across Indonesia.

Why spatial insight

Development is spatial.

Climate risk, agricultural productivity, environmental change, urban growth, infrastructure, and inequality all happen somewhere.

PetaLab combines Earth observation, spatial analysis, and research communication to reveal patterns that conventional statistics may not fully show—and turn them into evidence people can use.

How we work
